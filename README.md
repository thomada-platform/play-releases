# Play

**App Name**
Play

**App Publisher**
Thomada Platform

**Category**
Desktop app · local video & audio player (Windows, macOS, Linux)

**Description**
Play is a desktop media player that helps you watch and listen to the video and audio files already sitting on your PC - without a clunky, dated interface standing in the way.

**The Story Behind It**
I'd just come off an hour of YouTube, smooth, fast, no friction, and then tried to open a movie file sitting right on my own hard drive. The player that came up looked like it hadn't been touched since 2005. Gray toolbar, tiny buttons, a UI fighting me the whole way through.

So I decided to build my own. My media collection was already a mess of video clips, music albums, podcasts, and audio files scattered across MP4, MKV, MP3, FLAC, and more. What I wanted was YouTube's layout, that clean, familiar feel, but for my own local files, running on my own machine, with nothing to subscribe to.

Today you open a folder or drag in files and Play handles both video and audio side by side. You build playlists, pick up a video right where you paused it, switch into a stripped-down Music Mode when you're just listening, add and style your own subtitles, and pick a light or dark theme to match your desktop. Once it's installed, everything runs locally - the only time it reaches the internet is to check for a new version.

I built this for myself first, to stop fighting with that old-school player every time I wanted to watch something local. I still use it every day.

**Download / Link**
[Latest release](https://github.com/thomada-platform/play-releases/releases/latest)
- **Windows:** Download `play-setup-v{version}-win-x64.exe` (or `-ia32.exe` for 32-bit systems). Run the installer and follow the setup wizard.
- **macOS:** Download `play-setup-v{version}-mac-x64.dmg` (or `-arm64.dmg` for Apple Silicon). Since Play isn't notarized by Apple yet, macOS will flag it as unrecognized - right-click the app and choose Open the first time to get past Gatekeeper.
- **Linux:** Download `play-setup-v{version}-linux-x64.AppImage` or the `.deb` package. For the AppImage, run `chmod +x` on the file first to make it executable.

Play checks this page for new releases and will let you know in-app when an update is available.
